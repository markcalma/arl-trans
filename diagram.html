How ARL-Trans Works

Automated email processing for Delivery Orders and Water Reports

Step 1 — Who sends the emails
📄

Freight Forwarders / NVOs

  • Send Delivery Order (DO) emails to Lorene
  • e.g. AMERCAREROYAL, USF
  • Attachment: PDF or Excel
  • Nothing changes on their end
📊

Amercare / ACR

  • Send Water Report emails to Gary
  • Attachment: Excel file (.xlsx or .xlsm)
  • Nothing changes on their end
↓
Step 2 — Email forwarding
📬

Auto-Forward to Shared Inbox

Lorene and Gary's emails are automatically forwarded to one shared inbox: ops@arl-trans.com
No manual action needed from the team. Freight forwarders and Amercare keep sending to the same addresses as before.

↓
Step 3 — Automation kicks in
🤖

ARL-Trans checks the inbox every 60 seconds

Reads new emails, identifies the type, and extracts all the data automatically. No one needs to do anything.

↓   splits into two paths   ↓
Step 4 — Two types of email
📄

Delivery Order detected

  • Container number
  • Consignee name & address
  • Cargo description
  • Arrival date & terminal
  • Extracted from PDF or Excel automatically
📊

Water Report detected

  • Full container list parsed from Excel
  • Destination, ETA, free days
  • SSL line & terminal
  • Hundreds of containers processed in seconds
↓
Step 5 — Team reviews in the dashboard
👩

Lorene opens the DO

  • Reviews all extracted fields
  • Draws redaction boxes over sensitive info
  • Stamps the ARL-Trans logo on the PDF
  • Marks as sent when ready
👨

Gary opens the batch

  • Reviews the full container list
  • Clicks "Approve → Sheets"
  • New rows highlighted blue for Jose (Dispatch)
↓
Step 6 — Result
📧

Redacted DO sent to carrier

  • Lorene sends the redacted PDF to the carrier/driver dispatched to pick up the container
  • Consignee info removed — carrier only sees what they need
  • ARL-Trans logo stamped on the document
  • Google Sheets updated automatically
📊

Google Sheets updated

  • All containers written to Load Board
  • Master Container List updated
  • New rows highlighted blue for Jose (Dispatch)

✅ Zero manual data entry · No missed emails · Google Sheets always up to date
